# Repository Guidance

## Brand Positioning

Enigmatic Partners is an AI/automation consulting firm. The public site should present the company as fast, versatile, scalable, and practical, with a premium but restrained visual tone.

## Core Delivery Stack

- React
- Node.JS
- n8n
- Supabase

Use these technologies as the foundation for automation and AI service examples wherever it makes sense.

## Service Framing

- Focus on business processes that are strong automation candidates.
- Position n8n as the automation backbone.
- Include AI services such as intelligent document processing (IDP) when relevant.
- Emphasize that solutions are bespoke, standalone, and built to scale.

## Design Direction

- Keep the existing theme and colors consistent.
- Preserve the current editorial, premium look.
- Prefer clear narrative flow over generic marketing layouts.
- Keep homepage sections outcome-led and proof-driven.

## Content Guidance

- Avoid references that imply a platform-first or login-first product.
- Keep copy focused on outcomes, reliability, speed, and operational value.
- When updating marketing content, mirror changes across supported locales.
